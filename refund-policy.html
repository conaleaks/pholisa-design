Refund and Cancellation Policy

Trading Name: PHOLISA DESIGN • Principal: Pholisa Z Ntombela • Payment Gateway: Paystack • Effective: November 2026

At PHOLISA DESIGN, led by Pholisa Z Ntombela, we are committed to providing top-tier professional graphic design, visual branding, social media collateral, and marketing design services. We operate with full dedication and clarity from our studio at 3 Kwaza Street, New Brighton, Port Elizabeth, 6202, South Africa.

All client invoices, deposit requests, and payment transactions are processed securely through Paystack (licensed PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway).

100% Satisfaction & Quality Guarantee:
We strive to deliver exceptional design work that drives real value for your business. This policy details your rights and our procedures concerning project cancellations, revision cycles, and refunds.

1. Nature of Custom Digital Services

Our deliverables comprise bespoke creative digital assets and graphic design consulting, which may include:

  • Original vector brand identities, logos, submarks, and brand style guides.
  • Custom social media graphics, Instagram story & carousel packages, and ad creatives.
  • Print marketing collateral, brochures, business cards, roll-up banners, and corporate presentation decks.
  • Packaging, labels, and commercial illustration assets.

2. Project Cancellation Terms

2.1 Cancellation Prior to Project Kick-Off (Full Refund)

If a client requests a project cancellation within 24 hours of submitting payment and before initial creative drafting, sketching, or design preparation has commenced, the client is entitled to a 100% full refund of all monies paid, minus any non-recoverable standard payment processing fees charged by the gateway.

2.2 Cancellation During Active Milestone & Draft Phase (Partial Refund)

If a cancellation notice is submitted after initial design concepts or preliminary drafting have been shared with the client:

  • Preliminary Concept Phase: If the client elects not to proceed after evaluating initial design directions and chooses not to engage the included revision rounds, a 50% partial refund will be granted to compensate for creative hours and labor expended.
  • Active Revision Phase: Once revision rounds have commenced or intermediate approvals have been recorded, deposits are deemed non-refundable.

2.3 Completed Projects and Downloaded Assets (Non-Refundable)

Due to the digital, reproducible, and irrevocable nature of high-resolution vector and graphic files (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF, PSD, INDD), no refunds can be issued once final vector assets and source files have been delivered, downloaded, or approved by the client.

3. Complimentary Revision Policy

To ensure complete alignment with your vision:

  • Each standard project package includes up to 3 complimentary rounds of revisions.
  • Revisions cover adjustments to font combinations, layout positioning, color schemes, vector scaling, and copy placement.
  • Requests for an entirely new creative brief or a total pivot in project direction outside the agreed scope are treated as a new design order and quoted accordingly.

4. Refund Request and Processing Procedure

To submit a formal refund request:

  1. Email pholisantombela@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request - [Your Name / Invoice Number]".
  2. State the clear reason for cancellation and specify the Paystack transaction reference.
  3. Our studio will review the submission against work logs within 2 business days.
  4. Upon validation, refunds are issued immediately via Paystack back to the client's original payment method (credit/debit card or bank account) and reflect within 5 to 10 business days depending on your banking institution.
