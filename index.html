Terms of service

I provide freelance graphic design services from Port Elizabeth, South Africa. You can contact me at pholisantombela@gmail.com, or at 3 Kwaza Street, New Brighton, Port Elizabeth, 6202, South Africa.

Services start at R250. Each project is subject to a written quote agreed with the client. The quote records the scope, total price, payment schedule, revisions, deliverables, and delivery dates. Work begins after written acceptance, receipt of the required content, and any deposit specified in the quote (standard 50% deposit).

You must have permission to use materials you supply and check factual information and spelling before approving a design. Changes to the agreed brief, additional concepts, or extra revision rounds require a separately approved quote. No additional work is charged without your prior agreement.

The project agreement sets out the usage rights granted in the final design. Full commercial reproduction rights transfer to the client upon final payment settlement. Third-party fonts and stock assets remain subject to their own licences. Client work will only be used in my portfolio with standard professional courtesy.

For a complaint or enquiry, email pholisantombela@gmail.com with your project reference, the issue, and the resolution you are requesting. These terms do not exclude rights or remedies available under applicable South African law.

Payments through Paystack links

I collect client payments securely using Paystack Payment Links. After we agree on your quote, I will issue a secure payment link for the agreed milestone deposit or balance. You will complete payment on Paystack’s PCI-DSS Level 1 certified hosted payment page.

Clients may pay using approved Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard), Instant EFT, or electronic bank transfer. Any deposit, instalments, and final balance are explicitly specified in your quote. I do not request, process, or store your card number, PIN, or banking security code on this website.

Refund & cancellation policy

You may cancel a project by emailing pholisantombela@gmail.com with your name and project reference.

If you cancel before design drafting work begins (within 24 hours of payment), payments for unperformed design work will be refunded 100% in full. Once work has begun, the amount refundable is calculated after deducting the agreed value of work already completed and any previously approved, non-recoverable third-party costs (typically a 50% partial refund during the initial concept phase).

If I cancel or cannot provide the agreed service, all payments for undelivered work will be refunded in full. If delivered work does not match the agreed brief, contact me with the details so we can arrange an appropriate correction or refund. Approval and delivery of final digital vector files conclude the project, at which point fees are non-refundable due to the digital, reproducible nature of vector intellectual property.

Send refund requests to pholisantombela@gmail.com, stating your project reference, Paystack payment reference, and reason. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method via Paystack and reflect in your account within 5 to 10 business days.

Revisions & digital delivery

The number of included revision rounds and the delivery schedule are agreed in your written quote (standard packages include up to 3 complimentary revision rounds). A revision round is a consolidated set of changes to the agreed concept (adjusting colors, typography, spacing, or layout). A new brief, extra concept, or work beyond the included revisions is quoted separately before proceeding.

Designs are delivered digitally by email or secure cloud download link in high-resolution vector and raster formats (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF, and JPEG) ready for web publishing or commercial printing. Physical printing and shipping are not included unless separately agreed.

Draft and final delivery dates are confirmed for each project. If missing content, late client feedback, or approved additions affect the schedule, I will communicate a revised date immediately. Final delivery follows your approval and final settlement.

Privacy policy (POPIA)

Pholisa Z Ntombela, working as PHOLISA DESIGN, is responsible for personal information used to provide freelance graphic design services in compliance with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). For privacy questions or requests, email pholisantombela@gmail.com.

These are used solely to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, deliver design services, manage Paystack payments and records, and resolve issues. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your project.

Your rights: You may contact me at any time to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. You may also raise a privacy complaint with South Africa’s Information Regulator.
