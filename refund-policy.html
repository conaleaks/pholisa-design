Consumer Protection & Dispute Resolution

Refund and cancellation policy

PHOLISA DESIGN • Principal: Pholisa Z Ntombela • Payment Gateway: Paystack • Last updated: November 2026

You may cancel a graphic design project at any time by emailing pholisantombela@gmail.com with your name and project quotation reference.

1. Cancellation Prior to Design Commencement

If you request cancellation within 24 hours of payment and before design drafting or concept development has begun, all payments for unperformed design work will be refunded 100% in full.

2. Cancellation During Active Milestone & Drafting Phase

Once work has begun, the refundable amount is calculated after deducting the agreed value of work already completed and any non-recoverable third-party costs. If a client chooses not to proceed after evaluating initial drafts and elects not to utilize revision rounds, a 50% partial refund will be issued. A deposit is not automatically forfeited.

3. Completed Deliverables & Downloaded Vectors

Due to the digital, reproducible, and irrevocable nature of high-resolution vector and graphic design files (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF), fees are non-refundable once final approved assets have been delivered and downloaded. Approval of final files does not remove your statutory consumer rights under applicable South African law.

4. Included Revisions & Delivery Guarantees

Every standard package includes up to 3 complimentary rounds of revisions to guarantee your satisfaction. If I cancel or cannot provide the agreed service, payments for undelivered work are refunded in full.

5. Refund Submission & Timeline

Send refund requests to pholisantombela@gmail.com with your project reference, Paystack transaction reference, and reason. Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original payment method through Paystack within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.
