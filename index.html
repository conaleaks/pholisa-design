How We Work

Transparent Client Engagement Workflow

From our initial discussion at Port Elizabeth to the final hand-off of vector assets, here is our 5-step collaborative process:

1

Discovery & Creative Brief

We initiate contact via email (pholisantombela@gmail.com), WhatsApp, or direct project form. We review your industry niche, target audience, aesthetic preferences, and deliverable specifications.

2

Written Proposal & Paystack Deposit

I furnish a structured project quote detailing milestones, deliverables, and timeline. A 50% milestone deposit link is generated securely through Paystack to secure your spot in our studio production calendar.

3

Concept Development & Drafting

I develop initial creative directions, sketches, and digital mockups based on the agreed brief. Draft previews are uploaded to a private client review gallery for your evaluation.

4

Refinement & Revision Rounds

Each package includes up to 3 complimentary revision rounds. We fine-tune typography, color grading, spacing, and compositions until the result completely matches your expectations.

5

Final Approval & Master File Delivery

Upon final approval and settlement of the balance invoice, full commercial rights transfer to you. You receive a structured download bundle containing all master vector source files (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF) ready for commercial printing and web publishing.

Studio Policies & Consumer Protection

Clear, professional terms compliant with South African commercial standards to ensure complete peace of mind throughout our collaboration.

💰 Refund Policy

  • Cancellation Before Work Starts: 100% full refund if cancelled within 24 hours of payment and before initial creative design work commences.
  • Cancellation During Draft Phase: 50% refund if cancelled after preliminary concepts are delivered and the client chooses not to use revision rounds.
  • Final Delivered Assets: Due to the irrevocable nature of downloadable digital vector intellectual property, no refunds are issued once final approved assets are downloaded.
  • Processing Timeline: Approved refunds are credited directly via Paystack back to the original card or bank account within 5-10 business days.

🔄 Revisions & Intellectual Property

  • 3 Free Revision Rounds: Every single design package includes up to 3 comprehensive rounds of revisions to guarantee your satisfaction.
